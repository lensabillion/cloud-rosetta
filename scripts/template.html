Drawn at the same scale, the three hierarchies show why service tables mislead. The containers do not line up, and the boundaries carry different jobs.

One object, or three

An AWS account is the isolation, billing and identity boundary at once. Azure and Google split those across a tenant, a subscription or billing account, and a project. Source

Resource group is a false friend

Azure's is compulsory and deleting it destroys everything inside. AWS has an unrelated object of the same name that is an optional tag query. Google has nothing at that level. Source

Only Google's network is global

A Google VPC spans every region and holds no address range of its own. AWS and Azure networks are regional, and only AWS puts subnets inside a single zone. Source

Nesting depth is tested

Five levels of AWS organizational units, six of Azure management groups excluding root and subscription, ten of Google folders. AWS, Azure, Google
