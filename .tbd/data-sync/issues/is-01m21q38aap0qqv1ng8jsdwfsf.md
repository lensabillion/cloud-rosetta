Product principle governing every page. A newcomer must get what they came for within five minutes. An expert must find something they did not know. Apply as an acceptance test on each chapter and each view.

## Notes

Review 2026-09-09: reviewer inspection cannot close this acceptance test. Pilot with at least three zero-knowledge readers and three practitioners. Beginners: within five minutes explain where code runs, where data lives, who can access it, and why a bill appears; target at least three correct explanations and one transfer scenario. Practitioners: within ten minutes find one previously unknown insight, verify its primary source, and solve a changed scenario. Record timing, vocabulary blockers, wrong mental models, and revisions. See Delivery Order and Learning Validation in docs/reviews/2026-09-09-learning-experience-review.md.
