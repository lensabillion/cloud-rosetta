❗Serious❌No honest equivalent
AWSVPC endpoint, gateway or interface
AzurePrivate endpoint, and separately service endpoint
Google CloudPrivate Service Connect, and separately Private Google Access
Where it breaks

Azure's service endpoint and private endpoint sound like synonyms and are not. A service endpoint keeps traffic on the Microsoft backbone while the service keeps its public IP and nothing enters your address space. A private endpoint puts a network interface with a private IP from your subnet in front of the service, so it becomes addressable inside the VNet and reachable from on-premises over ExpressRoute or VPN, which service endpoints cannot do.
